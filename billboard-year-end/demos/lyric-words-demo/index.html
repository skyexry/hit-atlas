Sample and methods

The sample matches US Billboard year-end chart entries to genre tags and usable lyrics identified as predominantly English. Its 149 entries cover 144 distinct songs. A song that charted in two years counts once in each year.

We remove matched recommendation banners, ticket prompts and embed footers before counting words. For each song, we divide exact word matches by its word count and multiply by 1,000, then average across entries. Word forms count separately. A song with no matches contributes zero, and a year with no eligible songs is left blank. Songs can appear under multiple genres.

Genre lists and bubbles omit common function words and vocal fillers. Common words are the ten highest-frequency words in the genre sample. Distinctive words and annual bubbles require at least two distinct songs. Manual search includes all words. Distinctive words are ranked by the difference in mean frequency from the rest of the selected period.
