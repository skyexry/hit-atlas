Which audio features move together?

Features, sample and source data

Energy measures perceived intensity, danceability describes rhythmic suitability for dancing, acousticness estimates an acoustic sound, valence indicates musical positivity, and speechiness detects spoken delivery. Each uses its original 0–1 scale.

Tracks were looked up directly in ReccoBeats and checked against the chart title and performer credits. Genre tags come from the project’s Billboard dataset. Songs with several tags contribute once to each of their genres. Where several releases matched the same billing, we selected one with audio features, preferring an exact title and then the higher ReccoBeats popularity score.
